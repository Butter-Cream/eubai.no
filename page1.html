CHAPTER 1 - Introducing yourself

Greetings, "be" verb and introduction.

1.1 Sentence structure

Subject + Topic marker + Adjective + Object + Verb + Rest

    There are a few most important grammar parts:

    ʖ - topic marker

    na - possession

    lo - location (still)

    do - direction / destination (movement)

    ren - accompaniment

ʖ is spelt as "ayn", usually coming from the throat.
